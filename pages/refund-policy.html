Refund Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026. NextGen PDF 14-day money-back guarantee and cancellation guidelines.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all new Pro and Business subscription purchases. If you are not satisfied with NextGen PDF, submit a request within 14 days of your initial payment for a full refund.

2. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your paid subscription at any time via your Dashboard. Upon cancellation, your account will remain on the paid plan until the end of your current billing period.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team at support@nextgenpdf.com with your account email and transaction receipt. Refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
