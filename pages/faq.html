Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about security, file limits, or AI tools? Find answers below.

Is my PDF document secure and private?

Yes! All file transfers use 256-bit SSL encryption. Processed files are automatically deleted from our servers after 1 hour unless saved to your dashboard.

What file formats are supported for conversion?

We support PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, JPG, PNG, TXT, HTML, Markdown, and PDF/A formats.

How does AI Summarize and Chat work?

Our AI engine securely indexes your document text so you can ask natural language questions, generate summaries, or extract key insights without uploading your file to public LLM datasets.

Can I cancel or upgrade my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. You can manage or cancel your plan anytime under Dashboard -> Subscription with zero hidden fees.
